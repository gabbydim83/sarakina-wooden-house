THE SARAKINA WAY

Small wooden house. Endless seaside moments.

Sarakina Wooden House brings you to the water’s edge. Spend slow mornings on the veranda, cook outdoors with a sea view and step straight onto the sand.

Inside, the compact wooden bungalow offers a bedroom, living space, private bathroom and air conditioning. Outside is where its beautiful location truly shines.
